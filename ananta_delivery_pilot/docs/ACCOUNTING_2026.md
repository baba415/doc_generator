# Accounting Posture (2026 Managed Services)

## Ananta Flows Ltd (Merchant Principal)
- Books goods revenue.
- Books COGS, inventory/WIP as applicable.
- Holds receivable economics.
- Receives swept collections.

## Guildgate (Operator)
- Books service fee revenue only.
- Does not book full goods revenue in managed-service transactions.
- Uses dedicated collection account for assigned flows.
- Sweeps collections under assignment waterfall.

## Control Points
- No commingling of assigned collections with operating cash.
- No posting against non-adopted invoice artifacts.
- Manifest and hash records retained per transaction folder.
